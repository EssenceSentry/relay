# Demo Harbor service planning

SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — authored for Relay demonstrations. All names, dates,
quantities, and outcomes in these three documents are invented. This is not a
client project or a description of Blend delivery work.

## Goal

A fictional volunteer harbor coordinates arrivals, shared supplies, and berth
capacity. The demonstration replaces manual list copying with a scheduled
import and a human review queue.

## Scope

Use generated CSV records only. The three planning tools are Arrival Forecast,
Supply Alert, and Capacity Planner. They propose actions but cannot contact
vessels, order supplies, or control equipment.

## Illustrative targets

Process 36 generated arrival records per day. Reduce a fictional planning cycle
from 60 to 10 minutes. These are scenario assumptions; no real results have
been measured.

## Open question

The owner and subject-matter expert for each tool are not recorded. See
`steering-notes.md`; do not infer people from the project description.
